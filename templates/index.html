MindMate

A little support for your everyday student life.

MindMate is a private place to talk through everyday college stress, organise your studies, keep track of how you feel, and take small breaks that may help you reset.

What you can do here

Supportive chat

Describe what's on your mind. A small program on this computer makes an approximate guess about the topic (like exams or time management) and replies with a practical suggestion.

Study planner

Add tasks and deadlines, see what is overdue, and tick things off as you go.

Mood tracker

Note how you feel each day and look back at your own patterns. It's for reflection only.

Stress-relief activities

Guided breathing, a five-senses grounding exercise and private journaling. They are optional and may not work for everyone.

Your dashboard

Simple charts built from your own entries, so you can see your mood and tasks at a glance.

Wellness resources

Study tips and information on when and how to reach a trained professional.

Privacy note

  • Your chat messages are not saved and are never sent to an outside AI service.
  • Tasks, moods and journal entries are saved in a database on the computer running MindMate and are visible only to your account.
  • Nothing is shared with colleges or third parties, and your entries are never used to train the chat model.
  • Local storage is not a guarantee of complete security. Don't write anything here that you wouldn't want someone with access to this computer to see.

Please note

MindMate is not a substitute for professional support. It does not diagnose or treat any condition, and it cannot monitor you or guarantee that it will notice every difficult message.

If you are struggling, please talk to someone you trust or a qualified professional. See the resources page.
