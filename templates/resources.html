Wellness resources

General ideas that many students find useful. They are not guaranteed to work for everyone and are not treatment.

Managing study stress

  • Break big tasks into small steps and start with the smallest.
  • Study in short blocks (for example 25 minutes) with a break in between.
  • Keep a regular sleep routine and eat regular meals where you can.
  • Try not to compare your progress with other people's.
  • Ask teachers or classmates when you are unsure what matters most.

Breaks and organising work

  • Write down deadlines in one place, such as the study planner.
  • Move around, drink water or step outside during breaks.
  • Put your phone out of reach while you study.
  • Plan for rest. A realistic plan includes free time.

Talking to people you trust

  • A friend, family member, mentor, hostel warden or class teacher can often help.
  • You don't need a perfect explanation. "I've been finding things hard lately" is enough to begin.
  • Many colleges have a counselling cell. Ask your student affairs office.

When to see a professional

Consider contacting a qualified mental health professional or doctor if difficult feelings last for weeks, get in the way of sleep, eating, studying or daily life, or you simply feel you need more support. You don't have to wait until things are "bad enough".
