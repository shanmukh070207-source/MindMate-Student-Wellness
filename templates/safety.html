Safety information

MindMate is a simple wellness tool. It is not an emergency service, it does not monitor you, and it cannot promise to notice every message that signals a crisis.

If you might be in danger

Please contact your local emergency services right away, or reach out to someone near you: a friend, family member, hostel warden or college counsellor.

How the chat handles difficult messages

Before the chat tries to work out a topic, a separate, simple check looks for certain phrases that may suggest someone is in danger. If one is found, MindMate shows a support message instead of a normal reply. This check is deliberately cautious and can be wrong in both directions.

MindMate's topic guesses are approximate and are never a diagnosis or a psychological assessment.
